# MPPI Offboard Control

`mppi_offboard_node` executes timestamped local horizons generated by
`production_mppi_node`.

## Takeoff

Before navigation, offboard sends a position setpoint `takeoff_climb_m`
(2.0 m by default) above the spawn point in every scenario. The climb is a
real climb on purpose: a vehicle that never leaves its rest never lets the EKF
certify a control-grade heading, and the planner has no body axis to seed its
proprioceptive footprint from. When the spawn support sits below the flight
envelope floor (a street at `z = 0` with a `2.0 m` floor), the takeoff ends at
the floor plus the `0.5 m` capture tolerance instead, because the planner
rejects a route request whose start is below the floor. Takeoff completion is
latched after altitude capture and the configured hover interval.

## Horizon Execution

Each accepted horizon is validated for:

- increasing sequence;
- `map` frame;
- at least two finite points;
- a valid time window;
- every point and hold target inside `1.0 <= z < 32.0 m`;
- a finite stationary-hold target when requested.

At 50 Hz, offboard selects the horizon time at:

```text
now - valid_from + control_lookahead
```

It interpolates velocity, acceleration, vertical motion, yaw, and yaw rate
between neighboring samples and publishes a PX4 trajectory setpoint.

## Applied-Control Feedback

The acceleration and yaw-rate command actually selected by offboard is
published back to MPPI. The next MPPI update uses it to limit the first control
and avoid command discontinuities.

## Expired Or Missing Horizons

If no fresh horizon exists:

- moving vehicle: dynamically decelerate;
- nearly stopped vehicle: hold current position.

The old horizon is not extended beyond its validity deadline.

A planned horizon that reached its deadline is held at its last point only
if it was flown: a setpoint of it went to the autopilot while it was fresh.
A horizon received on the pad and outlived by the takeoff ends where the
vehicle never went, and is held where the vehicle stands (r4181).

## The Failsafe Ladder

What the offboard does when something is not right is one ladder
(specification K90), each rung entered and left by what is measured:

| Rung | Entered when | What the vehicle does | Left when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hover | a planned horizon is held past its renewal promise, a horizon is revoked or has lapsed, the planner's heartbeat is older than 1.0 s, the position source says nothing or dead reckons in the dark | brakes at the stack's deceleration and holds where it stopped; level, in the velocity mode, where no position can be trusted | the next horizon is accepted, the heartbeat heard, the source seen |
| 2. Landing where it stands | the planner's heartbeat has been lost for 5.0 s (also the mission's end and the operator's `land`) | holds the position where the landing began and descends at 0.5 m/s; level on the ground until the autopilot disarms | the planner is heard again before the ground (a lost planner's landing only) |
| 3. Landing without an estimate | the position source has dead reckoned for 3.0 s: declared, or heard once and silent for 0.5 s | descends level at 0.5 m/s in the velocity mode, no position asked; the ring's reflex where a ring is carried | the source is sound again before the vehicle is down |
| 4. Motors off | the vehicle is on the ground (the autopilot's disarm on its land detector), or the pilot kills | nothing more is sent | never |

The stack commands no mode and no arming for any of it; the pilot's choice of
mode and the autopilot's own failsafe take the vehicle from any rung
(specification K29). A flight enters the rungs on purpose with
`PILOT_EVENTS`: `fault:stop:production_mppi_node@T` hangs the planner,
`fault:stop:lidar_inertial_odometry_node@T` or
`fault:stop:visual_inertial_odometry_node@T` the estimator,
`fault:resume:NODE@T` lets it go on, `kill@T` is the pilot's.

## The Stack As A Mode Of PX4's Own

With the launch's `px4_external_mode` (`PX4_EXTERNAL_MODE=true`) the offboard
node registers the mode "Drone City Nav" with PX4 through px4-ros2-interface-lib
(specification K91), and the evaluation pilot selects it in place of offboard
(`sim_pilot.py --external-mode`). The pilot switches it off with any other mode
(`PILOT_EVENTS` `hold@T`, `posctl@T`), and the stack holds its stream as it does
when released from offboard. The setpoints are the same; the control they are
flown by is stated once per change with the flags PX4 derives for offboard. The
default stays offboard until the Urban acceptance has been flown through the
mode.

## The Renewal Promise

A fresh planned horizon is flown only while the planner renews it
(specification K89). Every planned horizon carries `renewal_promise_ns`: how
long after it the planner publishes the next, one and a half times the
longest of its last 25 intervals between planned horizons and 0.2 s at
least. The speeds of the horizon were planned for that long a reaction. A
planned horizon the offboard has held longer than its promise without
another is braked by the same local hold (`HORIZON_RENEWAL overdue=true`),
and the next horizon that comes is flown as any other: a planner that
refused its candidate, or took half a second over a tick, no longer leaves
the vehicle flying the old horizon into what was seen after it (r4150,
r4176, r4182). `tools/renewal_promise.py` reads a flight for it.

## Constrained Route Execution

Static `RouteSample3D` references carry altitude and speed constraints along
derived air-passage spans. MPPI follows the same typed route continuously;
there is no separate passage hold or nearest-opening selector.

## Vehicle Destruction

A typed `VehicleDestroyed` event stops setpoint execution and latches bounded
PX4 force-disarm retries until the vehicle status confirms disarmed. The only
accepted causes are Gazebo `physical_collision` and mission-referee
`proximity_collision`; vehicle ID, role, and mission epoch must match the
offboard instance.
Mission failure, goal arrival, stale state, and planner collision diagnostics do
not publish destruction and cannot request force-disarm.

## Removed Legacy Control

The current offboard node does not implement:

- long-path projection tracking;
- P/D cross-track control;
- curvature feedforward;
- path continuity stitching;
- path-id ACKs;
- velocity terminal capture;
- pending successor or after-hold activation.
